About

Background

Before my PhD, I spent about five years in tax, in both public accounting and industry. I started my career at Deloitte, one of the Big Four, in Business Tax Services for real estate clients.

My research focuses on the accounting profession and the people in it. One project uses survey data to ask how students respond to the alternative CPA licensure pathway, which lowers the education requirement. Another uses archival data to examine whether cuts to federal tax staff affect employment at state tax agencies.

At Virginia Tech I teach as well as research. I have taught two sections of managerial accounting and led quiz reviews for a tax course, and I have attended workshops on communicating research to broader audiences.

Outside of work, I enjoy hiking and I'm part of a sailing club.

Accounting Student Decisions Following the Alternative CPA Licensure Pathway: Survey Evidence on Master's Programs and CPA Exam Intentions

This paper surveys accounting undergraduates, graduate students and master's program directors. It examines students' planned enrollment in master's programs and planned CPA exam taking following the reduction in education requirements under the alternative licensure pathway.
